## What I work on

- **Machine learning for drug response.** Graph neural networks and classical ML to predict compound activity across cancer cell lines.
- **Transcriptomics for mechanism of action.** RNA-seq processing and network analysis to generate testable hypotheses about how compounds act.
- **Quantitative cell imaging.** Label-free holotomography and fluorescence microscopy, with image analysis pipelines for phenotypic profiling.
- **Wet lab.** Cancer cell culture, antiproliferative screening (SRB assay), immunofluorescence and DAPI-based cell cycle analysis.

Most of my research code stays private until the associated work is published; public releases will appear here alongside their papers.
